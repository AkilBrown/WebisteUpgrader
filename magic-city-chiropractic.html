Meet Dr. Scott C. Albano

A proud Birmingham native, Dr. Albano combines deep local roots with world-class expertise. He earned his Doctorate in Chiropractic Orthopedics from LIFE University and holds Board Certification.

As a published author and 2004 Arnold Classic World Champion Powerlifter (and Alabama Powerlifting Hall of Fame member), he brings rare insight into lifting mechanics, athletic performance, and the demands of real life.

At Magic City Chiropractic he delivers quality over quantity — taking the time to understand each patient, explain every step, and create a true plan for lasting wellness.
